Men's Health

Testosterone Therapy Pearland

3J Urgent Care offers testosterone therapy for men experiencing symptoms of low testosterone.

Low Testosterone May Cause

  • fatigue
  • low energy
  • decreased libido
  • reduced muscle strength
  • weight gain
  • difficulty concentrating

Treatment May Include

  • testosterone level testing
  • laboratory evaluation
  • testosterone replacement therapy
  • ongoing monitoring

Medical evaluation is required before treatment.
