3J Urgent Care & Family Medicine

3J Urgent Care & Family Medicine in Pearland, TX provides fast, affordable walk-in urgent and primary care for adults and children. We accept most major insurance plans, including Blue Cross Blue Shield (BCBS), Medicare, Medicaid, and Texas Children’s Health Plan. Self-pay options are available.

We treat illnesses and injuries including stitches, colds, asthma, rashes, UTIs, STIs, ear, nose, and eye conditions, vomiting, dehydration, urinary retention, pain, hypertension, diabetes, and high cholesterol. We offer onsite X-rays, ultrasound, bladder scan, EKG, slit-lamp eye exams, and in-house labs. Services include IV therapy, physicals, DOT exams, women’s health, and weight loss with GLP-1.
